## Explicitly prohibited claims

The following classifications remain `prohibited` until evidence and the matching acceptance gates exist: official MAIC score; real-handwriting performance; impossibility of hallucination or invention; verified source-region grounding; universal named-human confirmation in the legacy MVP; full generic Chinese or Traditional Chinese support; production-safe multi-tenancy; PDPA compliance; official or audit-ready financial outputs; upload-ready MyInvois output; government integration or acceptance; pilots, customers, resellers, revenue or current margin; complete deletion; native mobile availability; measured civic or national impact; and the R1 workflow as a deployed product capability.

## Approval rule

The owner must sign the final ledger after the final pack scan, test evidence and portal rehearsal. Missing owner, provider, legal, native-language, accessibility, licence or publication evidence remains `unknown`; it is not promoted by a polished screen, a passing unit test or an owner assertion alone.
