# MAIC Nexus Challenge 2026 — Verified Facts

Researched from the official site on 9 July 2026. Sources: maicnexus.com/en
(home), /en/tracks, /en/terms, /en/faq, /en/application. Re-verify anything
marked ⚠ before relying on it; the site defers to its "Rules & Regulations
v2.1" which resolves to the /en/terms page.

## What MAIC is
- **Malaysia AI Conference & Challenge** — inaugural 2026 edition; "Malaysia's
  first 3-in-1 National AI Platform" (Conference + Challenge + Incubation).
- Organisers: Solara Global Media, Solarvest Innovation Lab, Huntier AI.
  Endorsed by MTDC; incubation by HATI; cloud by AWS.

## Our track
- **T5 — "AI for Public Services & Smart Cities"**: citizen service
  automation, mobility, urban data platforms, public-sector modernisation.
  Focus areas listed: citizen agents, gov-RAG AI, multilingual LLMs, civic tech.
- Track locks at submission ("one team, one industry"). T5 partner: TBA.
- Minit's fit: civic tech + multilingual LLMs + citizen-service automation
  (eROSES filings, LHDN e-Invois compliance for citizen-run organisations).

## Deadlines & fees — THE CORRECTED PICTURE
- Application window: **11 June → 31 August 2026, 23:59 MYT** — hard close.
- **Capped at 300 teams, first-come first-served** — late = possibly locked out.
- Registration fee: **RM 150/team until 15 July** → RM 250 (16 Jul–8 Aug) →
  RM 500 (from 9 Aug).
- **There is NO official 14 July deck deadline.** Our internal 14 July target
  stands anyway: it locks the RM 150 fee, beats the team cap, and keeps
  BUILD_PLAN discipline. But it is a target, not a cliff.
- After submission: preliminary judging online **Sept 2026** (results
  end-Sept) → Semi-Final Demo Day, KL, **Oct 2026** (top 10) → Grand Final +
  AI Forum + Gala, KL, **Nov 2026**.

## What must be submitted (portal: maicnexus.com/en/application, 6-step form)
Mandatory at application:
1. **Pitch deck — PDF** (no page/slide limit published ⚠ email support@maicnexus.com)
2. **Written project summary** (no word limit published ⚠)
3. **AI usage disclosure statement** (see `ai-usage-disclosure.md`)

Optional, addable/updatable from the team dashboard until the judging window:
- Demo video URL (no length limit published ⚠)
- Artifact / repo link
- Member LinkedIn profiles

**All materials and presentations in ENGLISH only.** (Product screenshots
showing the BM interface are our authenticity evidence — keep captions in
English.)

## Artifact rules (matters for Phase 7)
- The artifact link **must be publicly accessible, no login wall, during the
  judging window** — private/gated = treated as non-submission.
- Repo artifacts need **≥3 commits over ≥2 calendar days** dated before the
  submission deadline. (Our per-phase commits satisfy this easily — but the
  repo must be made public for judging if we submit it; alternative: submit
  only the live Vercel URL + demo video as artifacts.)

## Eligibility
- Team of **1–5**; **at least one Malaysian citizen (MyKad holder)**.
- One person = one team only (duplicates can disqualify both teams).
- Member changes need organiser approval between stages.

## Judging rubric (100 points, 3 independent judges, scores normalised)
| Criterion | Weight | What Minit shows |
|---|---|---|
| Technical Feasibility | 25% | Working hero workflow on real handwriting; accuracy number; deterministic money architecture |
| Commercial Viability | 25% | **Weakest spot — needs a real business-model slide, not a placeholder** |
| Industry Relevance | 20% | eROSES/ROS + LHDN e-Invois are live national digitalisation pushes; T5 civic-tech fit |
| Scalability | 15% | HQ+branches tree; same rails serve every society type; 300k+ orgs story [VERIFY count] |
| ESG / National Impact | 15% | Governance for charities: audit-able donations, PDPA by design, compliance inclusion for non-English-speaking volunteers |

## Prizes
- Champion: RM 100k cash + RM 100k equity (= RM 200k) + HATI incubation.
- 1st runner-up RM 100k; 2nd runner-up RM 50k; 5 × RM 5k category awards.
- All accepted teams: AWS credits + architect support.
- ⚠ Top-3 condition: incorporate a Malaysian Sdn. Bhd. (with Malaysian
  director) within 90 days of award notification; equity onboarding within a
  further 60 days. Cash to a Malaysian citizen's bank account within 30 days
  of the Grand Final.

## IP & data
- Team keeps all IP; organisers get a non-exclusive royalty-free licence to
  use submitted materials for marketing/editorial.
- Organiser handles participant personal data under PDPA 2010 (24-month
  retention; 36 for finalists).
